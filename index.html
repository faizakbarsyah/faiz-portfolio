04 / ABOUT & MINDSET

Designing with clarity, narrative discipline, and strategic intent.

I am a multidisciplinary designer and creative director based in Medan, Indonesia. My work spans brand identity, motion graphics, spatial event visual systems, and commercial art direction.

Rather than approaching design purely as visual decoration, I focus on how visual languages function in real-world contexts — whether directing stage LED displays for corporate gatherings, structuring campaign narrative frameworks, or crafting precision motion systems.
